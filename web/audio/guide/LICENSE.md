# Guide voice clips

The `.ogg` clips in this folder are dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

They were synthesised with [Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M)
(voice `am_michael`), which is licensed under the Apache License 2.0
(https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). Regenerate them with
`/root/.venvs/kokoro/bin/python tools/voice/generate.py`.
